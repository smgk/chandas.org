About Chandas.org

Version 1.14.1 · pre-beta

Chandas.org is a fun & intuitive tool to get hands on with indic prosody. It aims to be unobtrusive and provide feedback live as you compose (in Kannada and Devanagari for now). Chandassu is best learnt by writing poems, it is hard to look up rules for beginners and get their poem validated. Chandas.org aims to lower the barrier of entry and make writing poems fun.

A few years ago @amshumankr and I discussed the need for such an app, specifically to use in flame-wars on twitter, a simple rule "diss all you want, BUT in-verse" would make the world a better place. It was decided that I would implement it. Bought the domain immediately and wrote the base logic for prastāra, the rest.... had to wait for AI to catch up :-) ... Hope you enjoy it. If you do, send the link to others, and a tweet my way.

Thanks are due to many who have inspired this project without knowing. First to Shatavadhani Dr R. Ganesh for inspiring many into the world of prosody. Thanks to the people who shared knowledge on padyapaana.com(site unreachable) and sanskrit-metres projects. Fellow prosody enthusiasts on twitter and elsewhere, that compose on all things trivial and profound... this is for us, so our tribe may increase.
