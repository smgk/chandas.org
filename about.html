About Chandas

Chandas.org is a fun intuitive tool to get hands on with indic prosody. It aims to be unobtrusive and provide feedback live as you compose (in Kannada and Devanagari for now). Chandas is best learnt by writing poems, it is hard to look up rules for beginners and get their poem validated. Chandas.org aims to lower the barrier and make it fun. A few years ago @amshumankr and I discussed about such an app, specifically to use in flame wars over twitter "diss all you want, BUT in-verse". I bought the domain immediately and the rest.... had to wait until AI ... Hope you enjoy it, if you do send a tweet my way.
