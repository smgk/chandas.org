# Kannada folk Tripadi rule packet

**Catalog ID:** `structural:tripadi-folk-kannada`  
**Status:** Provisional folk mātrā frame; expert corpus review pending

## Why this is a separate meter

The existing `structural:tripadi-kannada` entry represents classical Tripadi
as the aṃśa frame `VVVV / VBVV / VBV`. It remains unchanged.

Later and folk Tripadi can instead be heard as the three-line mātrā frame:

| Line | Gaṇas | Sung total |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 1 | `5 + 5 + 5 + 5` | 20 |
| 2 | `5 + 4 + 5 + 5` | 19 |
| 3 | `5 + 4 + 5` | 14 |

Keeping these as separate catalog entries lets a poet deliberately choose the
tradition being checked. A folk realization does not weaken or silently alter
the classical validator.

## Inferred sung realization

A written Laghu at the end of a gaṇa may be held for one additional mātrā in
song or recitation. When that single extra mātrā is needed to complete the
folk frame, Chandas:

- keeps the syllable classified as Laghu in the written text;
- does not report it as a violation;
- shows a faint superscript Kannada avagraha `ಽ` after that syllable; and
- leaves the authored, copied, and shared text unchanged.

The marker means “one sung mātrā is inferred here.” It is an analysis
annotation, not a character added to the poem. Extensions greater than one
mātrā, elision, and other regional or melodic substitutions are not inferred
by this rule.

Opening dvitīyākṣara-prāsa is checked independently across all three lines.

## Characterization example

The following folk Tripadi fits the encoded frame with four inferred sung
extensions and opening prāsa on `ಸ`:

> ಕೂಸು ಇದ್ದ ಮನೆಗೆ ಬೀಸಣಿಗೆ ಯಾತಕ  
> ಕೂಸು ಕಂದಯ್ಯ ಒಳಹೊರಗ ಆಡಿದರ  
> ಬೀಸಣಿಗೆ ಗಾಳಿ ಬೀಸ್ಯಾವ

The same verse remains subject to the classical aṃśa rules when the poet
explicitly selects classical `tripadi (Kannada)`.
