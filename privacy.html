Privacy

Effective July 26, 2026.

Chandas analyzes your composition on your device. The website and Android app do not send composition text to a Chandas server.

Local saved poems and recovery

Your compositions, per-stanza meter selections, templates, interface language, and editor position are stored in private application or browser storage on your device. New saves the current work and opens a clean slate; Saved poems lets you reopen, rename, copy, or delete local work. Clearing browser or app data can remove every local poem.

Save all to .txt makes a readable text collection and passes it only to a destination you choose. Full backup makes a portable JSON copy that can restore complete editor state. Both files contain plain-text private writing, so protect them accordingly. Restore merges safely: a differing poem with the same identifier is kept as a separate copy instead of overwriting work.

Sharing

Sharing happens only when you initiate it. Text is passed to your operating system, browser, or chosen social destination, whose own privacy policy then applies.

No Chandas cloud account

Chandas has no poem server, hosted database, Google login, Drive API, or composition telemetry. It does not upload or synchronize saved poems. A file leaves Chandas only when you explicitly share, download, or export it to a destination you choose.
