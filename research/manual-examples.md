# Manual poem-example research inbox

Use this file to collect authentic examples for Kannada-specific meters that
do not yet have a reviewed example in the Chandas.org Learn field guide.

Preserve the spelling and line breaks found in the source. A source URL should
preferably point to the exact Wikisource page or Internet Archive scan page,
not merely a search result. If any detail is uncertain, write `unknown` and
explain the uncertainty under Notes. More than one candidate is welcome: copy
the candidate block within that meter's section.

Do not repair a poem to make it scan. Detector disagreements are useful
research findings and will be reviewed before an example is promoted to
`examples/field_guide_corpus.json`.
