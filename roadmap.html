Roadmap

What we hope to build next, in roughly this order. Plans, not prophecies—meters are strict enough already.

  • Review Kannada meter rules: promote Kanda, Ragale, Ṣaṭpadi, Tripadi, Sāṅgatya, and Akkara from provisional support using expert-reviewed examples.
  • Stronger guided composition: add family-aware Strong templates for reviewed mātrā, six-line, and aṃśa meters.
  • Complete structural meters: add accepted Anuṣṭubh vipulā variants and deeper Āryā-family rules.
  • Clearer feedback: improve explanations, accessibility, Indic keyboard behavior, and cross-browser rendering.
  • Android distribution: package and release the offline-capable Android app after the web experience settles.
  • Synonym suggestions: offer licensed, offline-friendly alternatives with meaning and metrical fit.
  • More scripts: add Indic scripts one at a time, with optional transliteration and script-specific tests.
  • Short analysis links: make shareable analysis URLs tidier without losing the private, plain-link fallback.
  • Optional accounts and sync: save work across devices after privacy, deletion, and conflict handling are properly designed.

Anonymous composition, original Unicode text, and offline use stay part of the plan throughout.
